About Us / Kona-Ba Ami

Assosiasaun Estudante Timor-Leste iha Romania (AETLR) is a student association dedicated to supporting Timorese students studying in Romania. Our mission is to foster cultural exchange, provide academic support, and build a strong community.

🎯 Our Mission / Ita-Nia Misaun

To create a supportive environment where Timorese students can excel academically while maintaining their cultural identity.

👁️ Our Vision / Ita-Nia Vizaun

To be the leading platform for Timorese students in Romania, promoting excellence in education and cultural preservation.

💡 Our Values / Ita-Nia Valores

  • ✓ Unity and Community Support
  • ✓ Academic Excellence
  • ✓ Cultural Preservation
  • ✓ Leadership Development
  • ✓ International Cooperation
