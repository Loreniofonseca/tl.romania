🎓 Scholarship Program / Programa Bolsas de Estudu

The AETLR Scholarship Program is designed to support Timorese students pursuing higher education in Romania. Our goal is to provide financial assistance, academic guidance, and mentorship opportunities.

Tetum: Programa Bolsa AETLR nian halo atu apoia estudante Timor oan sira ne’ebé estuda iha Romania. Objetivu mak fornese apoiu finanseiru, orientasaun akademiku no oportunidade mentoria.

📌 Available Scholarships

  • Romanian Government Scholarship

💰 Scholarship Coverage

  • Tuition fee coverage
  • Monthly living allowance
  • Accommodation support
  • Health insurance
  • Research funding (for Master & PhD)

📋 Eligibility Criteria

  • Timorese citizenship
  • Strong academic record
  • Admission to a Romanian university
  • Complete required documents
  • Meet language requirements (Romanian/English)

📄 Required Documents

  • Academic transcripts
  • Passport copy
  • Recommendation letter
  • CV (Curriculum Vitae)
  • Language certificate (if required)

🗓️ Application Timeline

  • Application Opens: February 16, 2026
  • Information Session: February 20, 2026
  • Application Deadline: March 30, 2026
  • Announcement of Results: May 2026

📝 How to Apply

  1. Prepare all required documents
  2. Complete the online application form
  3. Submit before the deadline
  4. Attend interview (if required)
