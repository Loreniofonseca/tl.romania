🎓 Romanian Government Scholarship

The Romanian Government offers scholarships for non-EU students through the official Study in Romania platform.

🔗 Official Website: scholarships.studyinromania.gov.ro

What Does the Scholarship Offer?

  • Full tuition fee coverage
  • Monthly scholarship allowance
  • Free accommodation in university dormitories
  • Medical assistance
  • Romanian language preparatory year (if required)

Who Can Apply?

  • Non-EU citizens
  • Bachelor, Master, and PhD candidates
  • Students with good academic results

Required Documents

  • Diplomas and transcripts
  • Passport copy
  • Birth certificate
  • Motivation letter
  • Europass CV

📄 Europass CV

Europass CV is the standard European format for CV used for scholarships, jobs, and university applications in Europe.

🔗 Create your Europass CV here: europa.eu/europass

What is Europass CV?

Europass CV helps you clearly present:

  • Personal information
  • Education background
  • Work experience
  • Language skills
  • Digital skills
  • Certificates and achievements

How to Create Europass CV

  1. Go to the Europass website
  2. Create a free account
  3. Fill in your personal information
  4. Add education and experience
  5. Download your CV in PDF format
